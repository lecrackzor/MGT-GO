Ticker Selection & Polling

Checkbox enables collection; Refresh 0 = automatic via Priority; drag ☰ to reorder.
What the priorities mean
Polling is shared across every enabled ticker, so the medium and low intervals widen as you enable more.
PriorityLive poll interval
High1s. An open chart forces this, and so does setting the priority to High.
Medium6s at 5 or fewer tickers, 10s at 20 or fewer, 15s above that. The default.
Low16s at 5 or fewer tickers, 22s at 20 or fewer, 30s above that.
Refresh (ms) overrides the priority interval for that ticker. Rate limits can raise any interval above these values.
Priority only affects live polling. An after-hours backfill always fills the whole session at full resolution.
EnabledArchive onlyTickerRefresh (ms)Priority
"Archive only" skips live polling: the ticker's data is downloaded once after the close from the end-of-day archive. Priority and refresh are ignored for archive-only tickers.
